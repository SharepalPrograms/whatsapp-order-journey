Order value → asset & cashback logic

The message and landing page personalize themselves using the customer's order amount. The order value picks an asset tier, and the tier's asset cost determines the 3% cashback shown — credited to their SharePal wallet with a transfer-to-bank option, since they've already completed their order and shouldn't need to place another rental to benefit.

Recommended audience

Since becoming an Asset Partner involves paying a meaningful upfront sum (₹52,500–₹84,500+) for a return over months, this campaign should target people who can reasonably judge that commitment against their own finances.

Target: salaried employees, working professionals, self-employed individuals with steady income.

Avoid: students, individuals with no independent income, retirees on fixed or limited income, Chartered Accountants, and Advocates.

This isn't a legal restriction on the product itself — it's a responsible-marketing default, since unsolicited "invest and earn monthly" outreach to people without independent income invites regulatory and reputational scrutiny even when the underlying offer is legitimate.
