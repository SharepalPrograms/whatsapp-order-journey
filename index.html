How the offer works

Everything the customer sees is driven by two values in the link: the order ID and the order amount. No asset is matched to the order any more.

WhatsApp message: static text, the same for every customer. “As a SharePal Asset Partner, you will get ₹5,000 cashback.” Only the order ID and order amount in the first line change.

Offer page price card: shows the order amount, an instant cashback of ₹5,000, and “You pay only”, which is the order amount minus the cashback and never below ₹0. The ₹5,000 is illustrative, based on an asset worth ₹1 lakh (footnote on the page).

Where the cashback goes: the ₹5,000 splits into “To your bank”, the smaller of ₹5,000 and the order value, and “Stays in wallet”, the rest, usable on future orders. Example: a ₹1,870 order gives ₹1,870 to the bank and ₹3,130 in the wallet.

Earnings calculator: the customer picks an asset worth ₹50K to ₹5 L in ₹50K steps and a time period of 12, 24 or 36 months. It shows the offer bonus (5% of the asset cost, one-time cashback) and the monthly earning for that period. The earnings summary card below it mirrors the same numbers. The earning rate for each period is set on the period buttons in order-offer-page.html and is deliberately never shown as a percentage.

Tech team requirement: offer bonus transfer

Current behavior: a customer becomes an Asset Partner first, then places rental orders. The standard 3% wallet credit is deposited after partnership starts, and is only usable toward future rentals.

This campaign reverses that sequence: the order is already placed before the customer becomes a Partner. Since there's no "future rental" for the offer bonus to naturally apply against, we need a bank-transfer option, but only under specific conditions:

1. Eligibility: transfer to bank is available only to customers who received this offer (order placed before partnership). It does not apply to standard Asset Partner wallet credit earned the normal way.

2. Not 100% transferable: only the offer bonus itself is transferable, not the customer's full wallet balance (which may include other cashback, referral credit, etc. from unrelated activity).

3. Amount and cap: the offer bonus is 5% of the cost of the asset the customer buys (₹5,000 on a ₹1 lakh asset), credited to the wallet as a one-time instant cashback. The amount transferable to the bank is capped at the qualifying order's value, whichever asset they choose. Anything above the cap stays in the wallet for future orders. This stops a small qualifying order from unlocking a large asset's bonus and cashing out far more than the order that earned the offer.

Tech team requirement: Talk to us (callback request)

The offer page has a “Talk to us” button that opens a one-tap popup. It asks the customer for nothing, because the backend already has their details from the order.

1. Endpoint: set CALLBACK_ENDPOINT at the top of the script in order-offer-page.html. While it is empty the popup only logs to the console and nothing is sent, yet the customer still sees “Request received”. It must be set before go-live.

2. Payload: a POST with JSON order (order ID), amount (order amount), source (“order-offer-page”) and requestedAt (ISO timestamp). No name or phone number is sent.

3. Backend: look up the customer and their registered mobile from the order ID, create the callback task for the support team, and return 2xx. On any other response the page shows “Couldn't send your request. Please try again.” and lets the customer retry.

4. Abuse protection: order IDs are guessable and the popup needs nothing but an order ID, so someone could trigger callbacks to other customers. Rate-limit or dedupe per order (for example, one open request per order per day).

Gaps worth resolving before build

A few open questions this logic doesn't answer yet:

Illustrative vs actual amount: the WhatsApp message says “you will get ₹5,000 cashback”, and the offer page splits that ₹5,000 between bank and wallet. The page footnote calls it illustrative, based on a ₹1 lakh asset, but the real bonus is 5% of the asset bought, so it differs for other assets. Should the message carry the same qualifier (for example “on a ₹1 lakh asset”)?

Ledger separation: does the wallet need a distinct ledger entry/tag for the “offer bonus” versus ordinary wallet balance, so the transfer feature can isolate it? If credit is fungible in the wallet today, this needs a schema change.

Partial spend before transfer: if the customer spends part of the offer bonus on a rental first, is the transferable amount now min(remaining bonus, order value), or does spending forfeit transfer eligibility entirely?

Multi-order stacking: can one customer qualify for this offer on several past orders and stack multiple transfer-eligible bonuses, or is it capped at one per customer regardless of how many qualifying orders they have?

KYC for bank transfer: does SharePal already have bank/KYC details on file for this, or does the transfer flow need to collect them fresh, which adds a verification step before payout?

Time limit: is there a deadline to claim the transfer (for example, 30 days from becoming a Partner), or does eligibility stay open indefinitely?

Callback consent wording: the popup says “By requesting a call back, you agree to be contacted by SharePal about the Asset Partner Program.” That line is placeholder copy and needs legal review.

Recommended audience

Since becoming an Asset Partner involves paying a meaningful upfront sum (₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh in the calculator) for a return over months, this campaign should target people who can reasonably judge that commitment against their own finances.

Target: salaried employees, working professionals, self-employed individuals with steady income.

Avoid: students, individuals with no independent income, and Advocates.

This isn't a legal restriction on the product itself. It's a responsible-marketing default, since unsolicited "invest and earn monthly" outreach to people without independent income invites regulatory and reputational scrutiny even when the underlying offer is legitimate.
