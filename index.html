Order value → asset & cashback logic

The message and landing page personalize themselves using the customer's order amount. The order value picks an asset tier, and the tier's asset cost determines the 3% cashback shown — credited to their SharePal wallet with a transfer-to-bank option, since they've already completed their order and shouldn't need to place another rental to benefit.

Tech team requirement — bonus credit transfer

Current behavior: a customer becomes an Asset Partner first, then places rental orders — 3% wallet credit is deposited after partnership starts, and is only usable toward future rentals.

This campaign reverses that sequence: the order is already placed before the customer becomes a Partner. Since there's no "future rental" for this credit to naturally apply against, we need a bank-transfer option — but only under specific conditions:

1. Eligibility — transfer-to-bank is available only to customers who received this specific offer (order-placed-before-partnership). It does not apply to standard Asset Partner wallet credit earned the normal way.

2. Not 100% transferable — only the campaign bonus credit itself is transferable, not the customer's full wallet balance (which may include other cashback, referral credit, etc. from unrelated activity).

3. Transfer cap = order value — maximum transferable amount is capped at the qualifying order's value, even if the customer chooses a costlier asset (or combination) than the one suggested for their tier. This prevents someone from using a small qualifying order to unlock a large asset's bonus credit and cash out far more than the order that earned them the offer.

Gaps worth resolving before build

A few open questions this logic doesn't answer yet:

Ledger separation — does the wallet need a distinct ledger entry/tag for "campaign bonus credit" versus ordinary wallet balance, so the transfer feature can isolate it? If credit is fungible in the wallet today, this needs a schema change.

Partial spend before transfer — if the customer spends part of the bonus credit on a rental first, is the transferable amount now min(remaining bonus credit, order value), or does spending forfeit transfer eligibility entirely?

Excess credit if they pick a costlier asset — if bonus credit exceeds the order-value cap, does the excess stay as normal non-transferable wallet credit (rental-only), or is it not granted at all?

Multi-order stacking — can one customer qualify for this offer on several past orders and stack multiple transfer-eligible credits, or is it capped at one per customer regardless of how many qualifying orders they have?

KYC for bank transfer — does SharePal already have bank/KYC details on file for this, or does the transfer flow need to collect them fresh, which adds a verification step before payout?

Time limit — is there a deadline to claim the transfer (e.g., 30 days from becoming a Partner), or does eligibility stay open indefinitely?

Recommended audience

Since becoming an Asset Partner involves paying a meaningful upfront sum (₹52,500–₹84,500+) for a return over months, this campaign should target people who can reasonably judge that commitment against their own finances.

Target: salaried employees, working professionals, self-employed individuals with steady income.

Avoid: students, individuals with no independent income, and Advocates.

This isn't a legal restriction on the product itself — it's a responsible-marketing default, since unsolicited "invest and earn monthly" outreach to people without independent income invites regulatory and reputational scrutiny even when the underlying offer is legitimate.
